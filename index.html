How runs are graded
  • Recovery runs: average HR 130 or less is on target; up to 133 a bit hot; over that too hard.
  • Easy runs: average HR 138 or less; up to 141 a bit hot; over that too hard.
  • Long runs: average HR 138 or less and HR drift under 5%. Drift 5-8% or HR up to 141: a warning; drift over 8% or HR over 141: too hard.
  • SubT sessions: rep pace within 5 s/km of the target range, and no rep over 168 bpm. Wrist-sensor spikes (a burst over 168 for under 45 s that falls back mid-rep) and strides are ignored.
  • MP runs: the marathon-pace laps within 5 s/km of 5:15-5:25/km and HR 163 or less.
  • Any run under 85% of the planned distance is marked short. Races are listed with their time, not graded.
  • Same rules as the weekly review (tools/weekly_review.py). HR targets follow the README's training guidance.
